  The states with only identities to the left / right are now marked by the summand labels ``'IdL'`` and ``'IdR'``
  on the virtual legs of the :attr:`~tenpy.networks.mpo.MPO._W` tensors, see :func:`~tenpy.networks.mpo.partition_W`.
