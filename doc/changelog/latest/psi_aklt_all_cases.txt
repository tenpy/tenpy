- :meth:`~tenpy.models.aklt.AKLTChain.psi_AKLT` works for all values of ``conserve`` (``None``, ``'parity'``, ``'Sz'`` and ``'SU2'``)
  and for finite and infinite ``bc_MPS``.
  The only exception is ``conserve='parity'`` on an infinite chain with an odd number of sites per unit cell,
  which raises a ``NotImplementedError`` until :meth:`~tenpy.networks.mps.MPS.canonical_form` supports a non-trivial total
  charge of the unit cell, see `#765 <https://github.com/tenpy/tenpy/issues/765>`_.
- For a finite chain, the ground state of the AKLT model is 4-fold degenerate, since each end carries a free spin-1/2.
  :meth:`~tenpy.models.aklt.AKLTChain.psi_AKLT` returns the following state, which depends on ``conserve``:

  - For ``conserve=None``, ``'parity'`` and ``'Sz'``, the left edge spin is up and the right edge spin is down,
    as in tenpy v1.
    This state has total ``Sz=0`` and bond dimension 2 on all bonds, but it is not invariant under SU(2).
  - For ``conserve='SU2'``, the two edge spins form a singlet, such that the state has total spin 0.
    It is the AKLT state on a periodic ring, written as an open MPS, with bond dimension 4 on the inner bonds
    and 3 on the bonds next to the two edge sites.

  A finite chain needs at least two sites; for a single site, ``psi_AKLT`` raises a ``ValueError``.
